Short films last approximately 15 seconds. The finale joins three newly rendered scenes into one continuous MP4. Completion pauses gameplay; ending or skipping a chapter film advances once. The finale returns to the victory screen and does not restart the campaign. Replay never advances a chapter.

Unlocked films are available in Messages → Videos and the pause/help cinematics library, including films earned by legacy saves. Finishing chapter 30 also offers the full archive. The private encrypted dossier includes compact offline copies alongside the existing audio tracks. The public game uses higher-quality 720p files, H.264 video and AAC audio.

Generation prompts and provenance: `art-source/campaign-films/manifest.json`. No private dossier plaintext or PIN belongs in source control.
